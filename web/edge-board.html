Reality check

  • Break-even at −110 is 52.4%. A 50.7% record loses money.
  • The de-vig math is identical for everyone — it is never your edge. Edge only exists when your win% beats the fair price.
  • On real games this model lost money at closing prices. A gap between it and the line usually means the market knows something the model doesn't, like an injury or a new starter.
  • Real edge lives in three places: the best price across books (shop the dogs), news before the line moves, and genuinely better inputs than the market.
  • Treat a green row as "look here," not "lock." Verify every play by hand.
